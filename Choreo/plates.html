Plates

The block you clicked is the block you landed on

You just watched two separate HTML files animate into one another. The black plate on the previous page and the black plate above share a view-transition-name, and the browser matched them across a real navigation. The only thing that made it happen was @view-transition { navigation: auto } in the shared stylesheet.

Reload this page. Press back. Press forward. Every one of those is animated, and none of it is being coordinated by script, because there is no script.

The page was always able to do this.
