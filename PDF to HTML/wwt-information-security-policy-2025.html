Information Security Policy Standard

World Wide Technology — 2025 policy awareness training

Scope and Applicability

Information security policies apply to WWT assets, including personnel authorized to access WWT information. Third-party service providers must follow relevant company policies as per their contracts.

WWT personnel include employees and others providing services or affiliated with the organization.

WWT information includes any non-public information about the organization that could negatively impact it if disclosed, altered, or destroyed.

WWT reserves the right to revoke, change, or supplement information security policies at any time without prior notice. Such changes will be effective immediately upon approval by management, unless otherwise stated.

Purpose

Information security policy awareness educates WWT personnel on acceptable behavior and proper use of WWT information systems. The goal is to prevent or mitigate harm to the company and its stakeholders, reducing human cyber risk.

This training is not exhaustive. For more requirements, refer to the relevant information security policies.

Goals of Information Security

Confidentiality

Protect information from unauthorized access and misuse.

Integrity

Protect information from unauthorized alteration or modification.

Availablity

Protect timely and uninterrupted access to information.

Acceptable Use of Assets

  • Protect WWT information by following company policies and standards.
  • Access or share WWT information only when authorized and necessary for your job.
  • Screen lock your computer before leaving your work area unattended.
  • Promptly report the theft, loss, or unauthorized disclosure of WWT information to Information Security at InfoSec@wwt.com.
  • Report lost or stolen physical assets to the Global Service Desk via ServiceBot or ServiceNow.
  • Avoid opening suspicious emails and/or attachments from unknown senders; report the email by clicking the “Report” button in Microsoft Outlook or forward them as an attachment to EmailAbuse@wwt.com if the “Report” button is not available.
  • Use generative AI only for approved business purposes, such as marketing, product development, research, or internal brainstorming.

Unacceptable Use of Assets

  • Do not use personal email accounts for WWT business.
  • Do not transmit or forward WWT information to personal emails.
  • Do not post on social media using a WWT email without authorization.
  • Do not make endorsements on social media using personal email addresses on behalf of WWT.
  • Do not access content violating WWT policies.
  • Do not engage in illegal gambling.
  • Do not introduce malware to the network.
  • Do not use applications to bypass security controls or abuse WWT resources (e.g., torrent/P2P clients).
  • Do not input WWT information into generative AI tools unless approved to do so.

Clean Desk and Clear Screen

For WWT information:

  • Store securely in locked drawers when not in use or at day's end.
  • Shred or dispose of properly when no longer needed.
  • Minimize printing and promptly collect printed documents.
  • Ensure unauthorized individuals can't see sensitive information.
  • Erase whiteboards with WWT information promptly after use.
  • Disable email and messaging pop-ups during presentations, screen sharing, and in public areas when possible.
  • Store passwords securely and destroy them when they are no longer needed, if they are written down. Use password managers with strong encryption and authentication if available.

Information Classification

WWT classifies information as Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted, and Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) when needed. See Types of Information for more details.

  • Treat any information created or received by WWT as Internal by default.
  • Label information, whether printed or displayed, according to its classification level.
  • Maintain a record when disseminating or sharing information, such as business emails.
  • When combining information with different classification levels into a single entity, assign the most restrictive classification to the combined entity.
  • Do not release WWT information to the public unless there is a valid business justification and authorization to do so, such as evidence for a third-party security assessment or the status of a major vulnerability.
  • Dispose of WWT information appropriately, for example, placing it in an authorized disposal bin.

Types of WWT Information

Public Information

  • Advertisements and brochures.
  • Published annual accounts.
  • Internet Web pages.
  • External vacancy notices.

Internal Information

  • Departmental memos.
  • Policies and standards.
  • Standard operating procedures.
  • Phone and email directories.

Confidential Information

  • Software code.

Restricted Information

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII).
  • Protected Health Information (PHI).
  • Payment Card Information (PCI).
  • Company data breaches.

Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)

  • Critical Energy Infrastructure Information (CEII).
  • Covered Defense Information (CDI).
  • Controlled Technical Information (CTI).
  • DoD Critical Infrastructure Security Information (DCRIT).

Password Management

  • Change passwords immediately from the default and if compromised.
  • WWT has preconfigured passwords to be changed at least 180 days, contain a minimum of 14 alpha-numeric, special characters, and not to use personal or commonly used words.
  • The use of an approved password vault or manager like as Delinea and 1Password is highly encouraged.
  • Do not reuse passwords for multiple accounts.
  • Treat passwords as confidential; share only with the WWT Global Service Desk if resolving a technical issue.
  • Verify your identity before requesting a password reset.
  • User-managed passcodes and PINs must be at least eight characters long and meet the requirements in policy.

For password resets related to any security incident, the WWT Information Security team reserves the right to reset a password immediately prior to sending notification to the individual, if the incident weakens the company's security posture.

Access Control

  • Access to WWT information systems and data is granted based on the security principles of least privilege and need-to-know. Access is authorized in alignment with assigned responsibilities to benefit the company.
  • WWT personnel with access to these systems are accountable for their conduct and behavior when utilizing such assets.
  • Employees may submit a self-service request through ServiceNow to create or modify an account; however, this request requires approval from their designated manager.
  • Assigned accounts should be used strictly for their intended purposes, adhering to permitted activities. Standard user accounts are to be utilized solely for non-privileged activities, while privileged user accounts are designated for conducting privileged tasks consistent with the type of account issued.
  • Misuse of privileged access may lead to immediate revocation and disciplinary action.
  • WWT personnel must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) to access WWT systems unless exempted by policy.
  • Submit a service request if an account is locked or disabled. This can be done by the individual or their supervisor.
  • Accounts are automatically locked after five failed login attempts.
  • Do not disclose WWT information or speak on behalf of WWT without authorization.
  • There is no expectation of privacy when using WWT systems; all activity may be logged, monitored, and reviewed.

Media Protection

  • Removable media storage (e.g., USBs and external hard drives) is disabled by default. To use it, submit a policy exemption request through the Self-Service portal.
  • If you use removable media storage, ensure it is encrypted. Additionally:
    • Label, handle, and store all media according to its information classification.
    • Before disposing of media, ensure no unauthorized persons can access the data.
      • For disposal processes, contact the WWT Global Service Desk.
  • Secure media until it is destroyed or sanitized using approved methods.
  • For lost or stolen removable media storage, report breaches or incidents immediately to CSIRT@wwt.com.

Incident Management

Reporting Incidents

Received a Phish?

If you receive any emails that look suspicious or you need our team to confirm the legitimacy of the email, please forward to EmailAbuse@wwt.com as an attachment or use the “Report” button within Microsoft Outlook.

Potential Data Breach or Information Spillage?

Immediately report to the WWT Incident Response team at CSIRT@wwt.com.

Computer Loss or Theft?

Immediately contact the WWT Global Service Desk.

Asset Management

  • Ensure the physical security of assigned assets, particularly when traveling.
  • Return obsolete, broken, or unwanted hardware to WWT Information Technology (IT) for disposal.
  • For hardware donation requests, submit them through the Self-Service portal to WWT IT.
  • Once donated hardware is wiped and reset to factory settings, the new owner assumes full responsibility for it.
  • Use only approved and appropriately licensed software on WWT-managed hardware.
  • Do not use personal software on WWT systems to conduct business activities.
  • All technology for WWT business must be properly procured, licensed, and managed by WWT.
  • Return WWT assets immediately upon termination to avoid penalties and liabilities.

Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM)

  • Enroll mobile phones for which WWT pays for the service in the WWT EMM program.
  • Do not use personal workstations for business activities.
  • EMM-enrolled devices must meet security requirements and be password-protected.
  • Rooted or jailbroken devices are prohibited for WWT business.
  • Do not use blacklisted software and applications.
  • Keep operating systems, applications, and firmware up-to-date.
  • Maintain a device that meets the company's technical specifications.
  • Devices that do not comply will be blocked from accessing the internal WWT network until they meet the minimum security requirements.
  • Personnel using personally-owned devices for WWT business activities are responsible for costs.
    • For mobile phones, purchases made through the Self-Service portal will be deducted from a future paycheck.
  • Mobile network usage costs cannot be expensed by personnel.
  • You are responsible for upgrades and backing up personal information on personally-owned devices.
  • Protect login credentials, such as passwords, to prevent unauthorized access to devices.

The WWT Information Security team reserves the right to deny or disconnect devices that are not in compliance with policies and/or cause significant risk to the company.

Information Security Awareness & Training

  • Complete information security training during onboarding and annually.
  • Application developers must complete secure coding training when assigned and every two years.
  • Not completing required training by the due date may lead to corrective action.

Customer Data Protection

  • Review the MSA and SOW thoroughly. Each teammate that will provide the services must also review both agreements.
  • Pay attention to restrictions and assumptions in the SOW. Contact Legal for any ambiguous language.
  • Understand data types in scope and permissible systems for use.
    • Non-regulated client data can be stored on WWT systems.
    • Regulated data like Personal or Health Information must be stored on client systems.
      • Any exceptions require approval from Legal, Information Security, and Executive Management.
  • Only print, screen share, screenshot, or forward client data emails when authorized.
  • Ensure the security and privacy of client data.
    • Assign a Security Lead for large projects to conduct regularly scheduled security awareness sessions.
  • Use only authorized software and services for WWT and client data. Submit an exemption request if client's software is required.
  • Use only WWT and/or provided client email for services. Personal emails are prohibited.
  • Notify WWT and the client within 24 hours when access is no longer needed.
  • Report any security concerns immediately to CSIRT@wwt.com.

Following these practices protects WWT's interests, safeguards our clients' information, and upholds our reputation as a trusted organization.
