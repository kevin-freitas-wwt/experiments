Artemis Graphics Standard Guide and Graphics Assets

The goals of the Artemis Graphics Standard Guide and accompanying artwork assets are to help establish the visual identity for Artemis and provide a framework for developing materials that enhance public knowledge of NASA’s work. All employees, commercial, and international partners should use these guidelines for Artemis mission efforts.

Artemis Graphics Standard Guide download:PDF

Graphics assets download:Box folder

Artemis Inspiration Guide

The Artemis Inspiration Guide is a document showcasing the Artemis brand personality, brand tone of voice, as well as serving as an introduction to the Torch Bearer Design System.

Explorations in color, layout design and composition, as well as creative use of photography and typography give designers and communicators several tools to inspire.

Artemis Logo

For complete rules (including lock-up with the NASA Insignia) and examples on how to use the Artemis logo, please see the Artemis Graphics Standard Guide.

The Artemis logo is available in AI, EPS, SVG, and PNG formats for print and digital use.

Artemis Patch and Pin

This logo version is specifically used for the patches and pins and in selective applications only.

Internal and external use of the Artemis patch and pin must reviewed and approved by the Artemis Brand Strategy Team at artemis-creative@mail.nasa.gov.

This will not replace the official Artemis mission patches.

The Artemis patch and pin are available in AI format for print use.

Download:Box folder

Artemis I Mission Patch

The Artemis I Mission Patch should be used in conjunction with, and not replace, the official Artemis logo.

Do not use the Artemis I Mission Patch in lockup with Artemis Logo and NASA Insignia.

The Artemis I mission patch and pin are available in AI format for print and digital use.

Download:Box folder

Artemis Inter Typeface

Artemis Inter is a display font. Its use is limited to large size text as one word (such as ‘Artemis’, ‘Adapt’ or ‘Visionary’) or a headline of up to 3 words (for example ‘Space Launch System’ or ‘New Perspectives’).

For complete rules and examples on how to use the Artemis Inter Typeface, please see the Artemis Graphics Standard Guide.

Download:OTF

SpaceX Starship — Human Landing System Artemis III (2 Crew)

(JPG)

This SpaceX render should ONLY be used in reference to Artemis III and/or the first human landing of Artemis. NASA has not contracted SpaceX for any subsequent missions at this time and should not appear partial by (1) using the alternate image showing the SpaceX Starship with four crew and the LTV rover, or (2) showing a SpaceX Starship render in content relating to all Artemis landings or missions beyond Artemis III. Image should always show, “Image Credit: SpaceX.” Caption where appropriate should read, “Illustration of SpaceX Starship human lander design that will carry NASA astronauts to the Moon’s surface during the first Artemis crewed lunar landing.”
