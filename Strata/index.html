Strata

A voxel world grown from one seed, with a procedural cosmos above it.

Each 16×16 column of ground is carved in a background worker as you reach it, so the same word always grows the same continents, forests and seas. Climb and the ground coarsens into heightfield tiles, the sky thins to stars, and the same seed hangs a home system of planets (some ringed with lanes that resolve into rocks), asteroids, ships and stations overhead, and beyond it a lattice of other suns, each with its own worlds, each rendered in more detail the closer you fly. Drop onto any planet, or an asteroid, and it becomes voxels under your feet with its own gravity; find the beacon on a ship or station and step inside.
